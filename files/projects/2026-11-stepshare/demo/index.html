Guided tour
Probe

Gap closed: the share of the FID gap between dense 1:1 training and generation-only training that an arm closes, paired by seed (mean and 95% CI). Probe: linear-probe accuracy at a layer minus that of understanding-only training of the same seed; "worst" is the lowest of layers 2 to 28 of the seed-averaged profile.

StepShare splits every AdamW step exactly into an understanding part and a generation part, by depth.

Shares are means over steps sampled every 50 steps. Dense and per-task Adam: two seeds on one cluster; StepShare-Adam: five seeds on the other. ρ uses minibatch gradient norms averaged over 1000-step intervals (geometric mean over seeds); per-task normalisation fixes ρ = 1.
