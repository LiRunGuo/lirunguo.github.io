TL;DR. Drift scores compare a video's ending with its opening, so a long video that stops changing scores best. In the KV cache, drift shares its directions with legitimate change, and correcting every direction freezes the video. ToneTether corrects only the predicted global tone of the drift, which lowers colour drift without a measurable loss of motion or new content.

Which directions, not how strongly

Design ablations on 128 development prompts (Self-Forcing, 240 s, H100), all added to TetherCache. Hover a point for every metric; a ring marks a significant loss against TetherCache.

Table view

Tethers on all 128 directions trade motion for colour drift at every strength, filter speed and gating. ToneTether at τ=1 removes as much colour drift with dynamic degree and flow at TetherCache's level; the same correction on a random or top-drift subspace does not.

How it works

A plug-in for TetherCache that edits only the frames entering long-range memory.

1

Estimate the drift

A Kalman filter in the whitened KV statistics separates slow drift from content that also changes slowly.

2

Remove only its tone

Five directions per value head predict the global tone; the smallest edit that cancels the predicted tone leaves the other 123 untouched.

3

Follow new prompts

After a prompt switch, a 5 s empty-cache shadow rollout moves the reference to the new prompt's tone.
